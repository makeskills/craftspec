The Discipline of Quiet Luxury

TENET I

The luxury of what is omitted

AI interfaces default to filling every pixel with cards, badges, and gradients. Quiet Luxury does the opposite — it celebrates the power of negative space to give focus and weight to what remains.

TENET II

Strict systemic tokens

Warm ivory, muted stone, and refined amber. Every colour property is bound to a declared root token.

TENET III

Typographic stature

Classical Roman proportions on headlines, paired with a modern geometric body face for unhurried reading.

TENET IV

Accessible integrity

Elegance never compromises usability. Every interactive touch target exceeds 44×44px for composed mobile grace.
